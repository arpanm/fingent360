## Rights and source semantics

- [PPFAS disclaimer](https://amc.ppfas.com/other-links/disclaimer.php), [HDFC disclaimer](https://www.hdfcfund.com/disclaimer) and [Groww disclaimer](https://www.growwmf.in/disclaimer) permit personal/noncommercial downloading but require written permission for public/commercial reuse. Public disclosure availability alone does not authorize storing and distributing a copied dataset through the website or APK. No such agreement was supplied or accepted in this work.
- PPFAS digital factsheets display named holdings and percentages. Names alone cannot supply an exact ISIN or a defensible AMFI scheme/plan binding. Do not invent these identities from fuzzy matching or treat a factsheet excerpt as a reconciled complete portfolio.
- [CCIL MTM methodology](https://www.ccilindia.com/web/ccil/mtm-prices) describes clean prices excluding accrued interest. Its traded-price/weighted-price/model hierarchy must survive ingestion; an MTM value is not an executable bid or offer. [Operational aspects](https://www.ccilindia.com/web/ccil/operational-aspects) describe curve/valuation worksheets, but the exact current bond-price file layout and redistribution rights were not established here. The linked eligible-security page returned HTTP 403. Do not claim a CCIL adapter or verified credit rating based on these methodology pages.

The ordinary [SBI portfolio page](https://www.sbimf.com/portfoliospage_new) populated its current monthly table after page load in the in-app browser. Earlier PPFAS/Groww/HDFC failed downloads remain accurate but no longer block every possible initial AMC adapter.

Read-only ZIP/XML inspection verified: one worksheet; C3/D3 identifies the scheme; C4/D4 stores the statement date in the1900 Excel date system; C6:K6 names instrument/issuer, ISIN, rating/industry, quantity, market value in INR lakh, percent to AUM, YTM, YTC and notes. Individual ISIN-bearing lines, zero-exposure sections, TREPS, current assets/liabilities, section totals and grand AUM are distinct. A separate derivatives table follows the grand AUM; its long/short direction and exposures must never be added as more cash assets. Later detailed derivative disclosures are separate from that portfolio table.

The reported market-value and percentage cells use two-decimal accounting formats. Raw XML numerical lexemes contain binary floating-point artifacts, including the grand AUM. Preserve the original bytes and round exact decimal lexemes to their verified displayed precision; do not pretend sixteen-digit artifacts represent provider measurement precision. Preserve quantity precision separately. No workbook formulas, macros, external links or scripts were executed.

Original reported data contain a material consistency issue: the net-receivable/current-asset reported weights differ from their market-value/AUM ratios. Keep both original amounts and weights, flag the source discrepancy, and do not rewrite provider values or normalize visible securities to100%. Reconciliation must distinguish rounding tolerance from larger reported inconsistencies and prevent falsely claiming a clean complete look-through result. The independent reviewer must see failures before publication.
