# Entered holdings and reviewed CSV (PORTFOLIO-001)

TEAM-002 connects saved holdings to [quantity allocations](../product/goal-allocations.md), [immutable record reports](../product/record-reports.md) and explicit [identity lookups](../product/security-identities.md). The directory has real OpenFIGI metadata and source editions; this does not automatically validate every entered ISIN, provide current prices, or transmit private holdings to the provider. Historical limitations below describe the original PORTFOLIO-001 slice.

Account-owned holdings are stored in PostgreSQL independently from the fictional virtual journey. The browser starts empty. At `#holdings`, enter a standard CSV, review parsed rows and total acquisition cost, then explicitly confirm complete replacement. Add, update and remove individual entries by editing CSV rows. A header-only CSV clears active holdings. Download the saved CSV and inspect immutable prior revisions. Account deletion removes snapshots and previews by database cascade. No broker documents, passwords or provider keys are collected.

Format: `isin,quantity,total_cost_paise`. Exactly three unquoted fields per row, no formulas or extra columns, up to 200 rows and 50,000 characters. Indian ISIN structure and check digits are validated, but an identifier is not matched to an instrument master: security existence and equity classification remain unverified user assertions. Quantities are exact strings with up to six decimal places; total acquisition costs are integer INR paise (INR 100.00 = 10000 paise), max 16 digits per row. Zero cost is permitted for gifted/unknown-cost entries and is not inferred. No floating-point accounting or rounding occurs. Duplicate ISINs reject; users consolidate lots and reconcile source totals themselves. No valuations, live prices, gains, tax lot treatment, XLSX or platform-specific broker parsing is claimed.

Preview is private, server-persisted, bound to account/current revision and expires after 30 minutes. Maximum 20 retained previews per account; expired previews are pruned on the next preview. Preview cannot mutate active holdings. Confirm locks the account/version, preserves a new snapshot, and records idempotent replay until preview expiry cleanup; competing updates produce 409. Raw CSV is not retained; validated parsed preview rows and revisions are. All writes check Origin and require explicit storage consent during preview. Source provenance is user-entered-unverified, not market data. Standard CSV export fields cannot begin spreadsheet formulas because identifiers and numeric strings are strictly validated.

## Guided entry and review experience

The holdings page now leads with saved totals and a holdings table. Manual entry is the default: enter an ISIN, quantity and total purchase cost in rupees; Add to draft converts rupees exactly to paise without saving. Edit/cancel/remove act on the draft, then Preview and Confirm persist the reviewed complete record using the existing account API. A partly entered row cannot be silently omitted from a review. Optional standard CSV upload/editor retains the same strict server parser. The selected file is read locally into the draft; only submitting Preview sends it to the account API. Saving never infers market prices or valuations.
