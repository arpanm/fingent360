# Product and source trust boundary

This file records the accepted product direction, not a legal opinion or confirmation of regulatory compliance.

The initial platform provides research, education, simulations and user-controlled monitoring. Personalised securities recommendations require the approved operating model from the blueprint's Gate 5. No order execution is included.

All material displayed claims need supporting evidence and freshness. Facts, forecasts, scenarios and inferred causal impacts must be distinct. An LLM may extract candidate facts or explain validated calculations; it may not decide trades or override policy. News and filings are untrusted input, including any embedded instructions.

Source use requires an explicit record of permitted acquisition, storage, derivation and display, with review date and evidence. Publicly accessible does not mean production-approved. Store secrets outside Git. Use synthetic portfolio fixtures only; never commit real holdings, account data, broker passwords or OTPs.

Before user data enters the app, implement identity, tenant ownership, consent, encryption/retention, isolated import handling and prompt-injection controls. Before recommendations enter the app, implement deterministic suitability, cost/tax/turnover limits, no-action comparison, versioned policy, audit reconstruction and activation controls.
