# GOAL-FEASIBILITY-001: explicit downside capacity

This completes the bounded DEV-009 risk-feasibility behavior without claiming suitability or investment forecasting. Owners choose a saved goal and explicitly enter a monthly affordable contribution ceiling, an interruption length within the goal horizon, and a minimum protected portion of their already-entered saved amount. Any of these may be unknown. The screen explains these are user assumptions, not inferred income, verified liquidity, insurance or financial advice.

Policy `downside-capacity-v1`: compare the unchanged contribution-only plan with a downside in which monthly contribution is capped at the entered ceiling, zero contributions occur during the interruption, and protected savings are not spent on this goal. Spendable savings = max(saved − protected,0); stressed total = spendable + min(monthly,ceiling) × (horizon − interruption). Report exact target gap, monthly over-budget amount and protected-reserve deficit. Unknown inputs give `unknown` with no stressed total; known inputs give `shortfall` if any gap/over-budget/reserve deficit exists, otherwise `within-entered-limits`. No probabilities, returns, inflation, asset sale proceeds or recommendation. All INR values use paise and BigInt.

Flow: My goals → Downside capacity → choose goal → inputs/unknowns → review unchanged/stressed totals and limitations → explicit consent/save. An immutable receipt captures exact goal version, assumptions and policy. It never edits goals/allocations/holdings. History is dated; changed/deleted goals do not rewrite saved results. Exact request replay returns the original receipt. A deleted receipt remains a minimal request tombstone and cannot reappear. Keep at most100 visible receipts; explicit deletion frees capacity. No automatic deletion or lifetime shutdown. Owned account deletion removes receipts/tombstones. Complete privacy export includes all visible records and an explicit deleted-request count. Internal anti-replay fingerprints/IDs are not exported; no deleted financial content is retained. These minimal internal tombstones have no expiry and are removed on account deletion.

API and local handlers share calculation and validation. Account lock precedes goal locks; final wall-clock session check follows waits. UI401 clears private data, generation guards reject late reads, failed saves preserve request/input for exact retry, deletion needs confirmation, and stale goal conflicts require reload. The component uses the existing account route and responsive forms, not a fictional dashboard. Tests are authored only; the user performs validation.
