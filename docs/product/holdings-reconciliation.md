# Reviewed holdings replacement — HOLDINGS-RECONCILE-001

A preview captures the actual owned holdings revision alongside the proposed manual/CSV/XLSX rows. The review separates added, removed, changed and unchanged ISINs, with before/after quantities and acquisition costs. Quantities are compared in exact millionths of a unit; costs and signed differences use integer INR paise. There is no quantity total across unlike securities, market value, return, gain/loss or independently verified broker reconciliation. A missing side is labelled absent, not an observed zero holding.

Before any removal, the user explicitly acknowledges removal of all listed ISINs. Confirmation binds to the durable preview ID and expected holdings version, checks current ownership/session after waits, and validates the saved reconciliation before writing an ordinary immutable holdings revision. Successful replay retains its original revision even after preview expiry or later changes. Legacy successful previews remain replayable; legacy unconfirmed previews require a new review rather than bypassing removal acknowledgement. No raw workbook bytes or filenames are retained. Existing CSV/XLSX parser/declaration provenance remains attached.

Dependency counts describe actual saved allocation rows and active holding research connections at preview time. A new holdings revision makes their existing version bindings require review, including unchanged ISINs; neither allocations nor connections are silently edited. Preview history/export includes the captured baseline/differences/dependency context, with existing account deletion and preview retention behavior. No migration is required: existing preview JSONB safely stores the additive validated review payload. Older arrays/objects remain readable for privacy and confirmed replay.

The UI preserves drafts on validation/network failures, blocks unsafe stale/expired previews, and offers an explicit new preview after reloading current holdings. A committed response followed by failed GET is a saved historical receipt, not permission to submit again or claim current holdings. Manual rows, CSV and workbook paths share this review. Device mode applies identical contracts in serialized local storage with zero API requests.

Acceptance: exact fractional and near-limit differences; all four categories; full removal/empty initial records; removal consent; provenance; actual allocation/connection counts; concurrent/stale/foreign/session-expiry/legacy/replay failures with unchanged unrelated records; confirmed export/deletion; draft/error/Back/keyboard/mobile recovery; real connected and local persistence. Authoring does not imply verification.

Read ordering uses a generation per current-baseline request: an explicit Reload or draft-preserving baseline refresh supersedes initial reads. A late initial response cannot reset newer editable input. Authentication denial invalidates outstanding reads. Preview creation rechecks wall-clock authorization after potentially blocked expired-preview cleanup before returning or storing further private context.
