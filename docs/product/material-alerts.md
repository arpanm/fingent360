# MATERIAL-ALERTS-001 — explicit thresholds for stored annual observations

This DEV-011/018 child extends the account Observation inbox. The existing latest-observation cards, read receipts and mute preferences remain available. A new Material changes section lets an account owner opt in to a percentage-point threshold for each followed India annual GDP-growth/CPI series. It is an observation-monitoring rule, not an investment action, consensus surprise or scheduled-release claim.

The threshold is an explicit positive decimal through 100 percentage points, with up to six decimal places. Comparison preserves the observations' up to 30 decimal places using scaled integers. Absolute difference >= threshold is material only when the year advances, both values exist, and the stored source was successfully checked within the existing seven-day freshness window. Equal/below-threshold observations and same-year revisions never create urgency. A same-year revision records correction context, regardless of its size. Period year, provider-updated date, retrieval time, successful-source-check time and comparison time are distinct; no future release date is invented. A future annual year or retrieval time, including a prior baseline ahead of the evaluation clock, makes the comparison unavailable. It cannot establish a baseline or material notice.

Enabling a rule, changing its threshold, re-enabling after unfollow, or unmuting captures a fresh baseline without backlog. If data is null or stale, retain the last valid baseline and mark reset pending until a fresh value is available. During normal explicit checks, evaluate the latest changed-year observation against the baseline, then advance the baseline for each fresh known transition, including below-threshold and same-year revision outcomes. Always inspect the highest stored annual year and its highest revision, including an initial null; never silently fall back to an older known value. A null/stale result keeps the original valid baseline. A regressed year is unavailable for forward comparison. Muted checks freeze baselines. Removing a follow removes its rule/current notice while immutable history remains; re-follow requires explicit opt-in again.

The owner presses Check stored observations to evaluate at most two indicators in one atomic batch; no provider request, hidden timer, email, push or worker is added. A material result replaces the one current notice for that indicator and increments its notice version. Acknowledgment binds the exact current notice version; the owner may explicitly reopen it. Revisions/unchanged checks retain the earlier notice and its dated comparison rather than relabelling it as a new change. Current source context warns when the notice's observation was revised. Mute hides active delivery while history remains accessible. Historical save/replay receipts are separate from current authoritative GET state. Every receipt also retains the exact captured source heads and successful-check timestamps, so its threshold/freshness basis can be reconstructed after later source refreshes.

Migration039 stores one bounded policy/baseline/notice head per account plus immutable sequence-paged event/request receipts. Account-row locking serializes configuration/check/acknowledgment/watchlist/mute transitions; authorization is rechecked after waits and before commit. Mutations require Origin, requestId and expectedVersion; exact replay returns the original receipt, changed reuse conflicts. Existing watchlist/mute writes invoke context synchronization inside their original transaction. No existing financial/source row is mutated. Account deletion cascades; complete Privacy download follows all retained event pages with owner/boundary verification and refuses partial output on failure or401. There is no lifetime shutdown cap or silent history truncation.

Local mode runs the identical reducer against actual dated bundle observations/history under the serialized local state bridge, with zero API/provider traffic. Baselines/notices/receipts survive reload and are deleted/exported with the account. Bundle date is visible, and disconnected mode cannot know later source revisions or checks. Annual-data context remains historical even when the app has just checked its stored bundle.
