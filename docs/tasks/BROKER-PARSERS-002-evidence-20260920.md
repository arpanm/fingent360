# Broker formats and connectivity — primary-source follow-up, 20 September 2026

Bounded public official-domain searches for downloadable XLSX/CSV examples, export templates and exact headers did not locate a complete original holdings workbook for the five named brokers. This is a research result, not proof that no public sample exists. Existing mapped/supplemented imports remain available; no parser is enabled and no private sample or credential was accessed.

Additional evidence helps distinguish unsuitable substitutes:

- [Zerodha SOH help](https://support.zerodha.com/category/console/portfolio/statement/articles/statement-of-holdings-soh-and-details-in-soh) defines separate current, free, locked, pledged and earmarked balances. It concerns a demat statement obtained through CDSL or a ticket, not the Console XLSX export. Those fields do not establish its workbook grammar or acquisition costs.
- [ICICI's current statement guide](https://www.icicidirect.com/ilearn/account-opening/articles/demat-account-statement-a-guide-to-downloading-and-reading-it) lists common demat balances and indicative value; its [portfolio download help](https://www.icicidirect.com/faqs/stocks/how-can-i-download-a-summary-of-my-portfolio-on-website) still describes a download action, not a complete versioned export.
- [Upstox's CSV announcement](https://upstox.com/market-talk/january-updates-trade-from-charts-watchlist-tags-and-more/) establishes export availability. Instrument-master CSV/API schemas and fund portfolio PDFs returned by searches concern other products and were not adopted as customer holdings formats.

The existing sanitized-template availability question remains pending; it is not repeated. A sufficient answer preserves worksheet names, preamble/header/footer, cell formats and quantity/cost labels using invented rows and no hidden personal metadata. A verified absence of acquisition cost would retain explicit user-attested supplemental costs; average price or market valuation cannot silently become exact acquisition cost.

Connectivity belongs to DEV-028/SRC-021, separately from SRC-013 file imports. They do not supply this application's delegated partner authorization/callback/revocation contract. Groww's [partner article](https://groww.in/blog/trade-on-algo-test-using-groww) and [SDK introduction](https://groww.in/trade-api/docs/python-sdk) mention OAuth; that is positive evidence of an offering, not grounds to invent its endpoints or state binding.

The [Breeze reference](https://api.icicidirect.com/breezeapi/documents/index.html) and [official SDK](https://github.com/Idirect-Tech/Breeze-Python-SDK) document obtaining API_Session and constructing authenticated requests. Neither inspected login description establishes a one-time caller-state echo or equivalent multi-user callback correlation. The existing partner eligibility/retention and secure-callback input checklist remains applicable. No personal-token collection product or undocumented OAuth path was introduced.
